Study Information

Purpose of the Study
This platform is part of an academic research study aiming to understand and mitigate "cognitive offloading" to Artificial Intelligence. Participants will be assigned to either an intervention or control group to evaluate the effectiveness of cognitive micro-games.
Data Collection
To ensure the integrity of the study, we collect anonymized gameplay logs. This includes typing speeds (Words Per Minute), vocabulary diversity (Type-Token Ratio), and paste events. Our built-in DetectifyAI system uses this telemetry to distinguish human effort from AI-generated text.
Privacy & Security
Your data is anonymized, stored securely in Firebase, and only used for academic research. No personal identifiers are shared. You may request data deletion at any time.
