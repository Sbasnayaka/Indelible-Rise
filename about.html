Why Indelible Rise?

We all use generative AI. It’s fast, it’s helpful and it’s here to stay. But recentlyyyyyy, 79% of young people admitted that relying on AI too much makes their own thinking skills feel a bit "rusty"!!!

When was the last time you solved a tough problem without instantly opening a new tab to ask for the answer?

Indelible Rise is a creative arcade built to fix that. We designed short, engaging micro games to give your brain a proper workout. Whether you are testing your memory, spotting fake news, or writing creatively, this is your space to grow.

Powered by DetectifyAI

To make sure you get the best workout, our background DetectifyAI engine gently monitors your gameplay. It doesn't read your answers like an exam marker. It just makes sure you aren't copy pasting from other apps. Your original thoughts onlyyyyy!!...
